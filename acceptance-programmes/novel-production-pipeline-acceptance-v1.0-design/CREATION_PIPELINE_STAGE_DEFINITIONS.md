# Creation Pipeline Stage Definitions

This document bridges Writer qualification to the future creation pipeline. It is not authorised execution.

## C1. Concept / market / reader-contract definition

Purpose: define what kind of book is being created, who it is for, and what promise it makes.
Outputs: concept brief, shelf/genre, reader contract, commercial/personal priority, constraints.

## C2. Architecture / showrunner design

Purpose: produce a flexible story architecture, not a rigid chapter prison.
Outputs: series fit, premise, character engine, conflict/stakes, act/beat plan, research triggers, voice target, stop gates.

## C3. Conditional research package

Purpose: gather only research needed for credibility and creative specificity.
Outputs: research dossier, claim watchlist, usable setting/procedural/cultural details, uncertainty labels.

## C4. Voice test and guardrails

Purpose: prove voice/POV/reader contract before full drafting.
Outputs: voice guardrails, sample scene/chapter, review package, approval/rework gate.

## C5. Writer drafting

Purpose: draft chapter/scene prose under the approved architecture and guardrails.
Inputs: architecture, job statement, relevant context, research snippets, prior chapter if needed.
Outputs: draft prose plus provenance and tracker update.

## C6. Draft assembly and integrity check

Purpose: confirm the complete draft exists and can enter manuscript production.
Outputs: manuscript source lock, word/chapter counts, known gaps, handoff to the manuscript production pipeline.

## Handoff contract into manuscript production

A complete draft may enter the manuscript production pipeline only when it has: canonical repo path, source file hashes, current status/handoff, model/provenance record where available, series/canon context pointers, and an explicit boundary between known draft flaws and authorised revision scope.
