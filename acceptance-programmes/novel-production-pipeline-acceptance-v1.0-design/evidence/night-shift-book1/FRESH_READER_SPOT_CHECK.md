## 2. Chapter 14 in Full (lines 2744–2838) — and the Ch 13 → Ch 14 Transition

**Reads naturally as a chapter:** Yes, its internal content is strong.
**Does the Ch 13 → Ch 14 transition work:** **No. This is the main issue found.**

### The Problem: Chronological Backtrack / Duplicated Handover

Chapter 13 ends with:
- 06:00, day shift arrives (Patricia, Sophie, agency nurse named)
- Mara gives handover ("Fourteen patients, all stable... Mrs. Park: seizure... Power failure...")
- "She didn't mention the basement. She didn't mention the door."
- Drives home (22 minutes), goes inside, closes curtains, sets alarm
- Sleeps, dreams, "And she would be there."

Chapter 14 immediately opens with:
- "Dawn. The day shift arrived."
- Mara gives handover ("Fourteen patients, all stable. Mrs. Park: seizure overnight, IV lorazepam given... Power failure...")
- "She didn't mention the basement. She didn't mention the door."
- Then continues into new content (Liam conversation, Gwen conversation, roof scene)

**The reader experiences the same morning twice.** The day shift arrival, the handover summary, and the "didn't mention the basement" beat are replayed almost verbatim. There is no scene-break signal, no timestamp, no narrator cue to indicate we're rewinding — it simply jumps backward in time from Ch 13's ending (Mara asleep at home) to Ch 14's opening (Mara still on the ward at dawn).

A fresh reader would likely experience this as:
- Confusion ("Wait, didn't she already go home?")
- Or déjà vu ("I just read this handover")
- The "She didn't mention the basement" line appears in both chapters nearly word-for-word, which compounds the effect

### What Ch 14 Does Well (Internally)

Once past the repeated opening, Ch 14's content is excellent:
- **Liam scene:** Emotional, earned. His grey face, the tears, "Thank you." The moment where he realizes he did the work despite terror — this is a real character beat.
- **Gwen scene:** Her confession ("I watched for twenty years... twenty-three patients died in Room 14") is one of the strongest passages in the sections I read. The moral weight is clear without being overwrought.
- **Roof scene:** The correlation-vs-causation reflection is well-handled — it doesn't resolve the ambiguity, which is thematically right.

### Recommendation

Either:
1. **Trim Ch 13's ending** to stop before the 06:00 handover (end on the charting / "Same time tonight" exchange with Gwen, around line 2715), letting Ch 14 own the morning entirely; or
2. **Rewrite Ch 14's opening** to not replay the handover — start with Liam at the station or a clear temporal marker like "Later that morning" that signals we're still in the same dawn, not restarting it.
