## PURPOSE

The Series Longevity Contract is a pre-production document completed before Book 1 planning. It records the structural and longevity decisions that define the series.

It is a contract with the series itself — a record of what the series is, what it needs, and what would damage it.

**Integration warning:** This contract is a Working Storycraft tool. Do not treat it as Novel Production System doctrine until the framework has been tested against at least two real Arden series and approved by Andrew.

---

## HOW TO USE

1. Complete all applicable sections before Book 1 planning.
2. Skip sections marked "if applicable" when they don't apply to your series.
3. Review after Book 1, before each subsequent book, and at natural milestones.
4. When a decision changes, record the change and reason.
5. When creative judgement overrides a provision, record the override and the trade-off.
