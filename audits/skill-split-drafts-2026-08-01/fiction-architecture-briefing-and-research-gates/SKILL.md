# Fiction Architecture Briefing And Research Gates

## Purpose

Control pre-prose creative planning: concept refinement, architecture, chapter maps, job statements, research dossiers, and controlled chapter briefs. This skill stops before prose unless the user separately authorizes drafting.

## When to Use

- User says “review and refine this request”.
- Creating or revising a book/series architecture.
- Building a chapter-function map or tracker.
- Writing a controlled chapter brief.
- Running research-only mythology/history/market/technical realism dossiers.
- Deciding what can enter canon from research.

## Non-Triggers

Do not use for actual chapter prose. Do not use for revision of existing prose. Do not use for reader-site packaging.

## Procedure

1. Read the current repo authority stack and relevant bibles/status/handoff.
2. Identify what is already decided vs what requires new decision.
3. If research is needed, keep evidence separate from canon adoption.
4. Write architecture/briefing documents with explicit reveal/withholding gates.
5. Update status/handoff/decision log only to reflect the planning artifact.
6. Validate no protected manuscript/prose files changed.
7. Commit/push planning documents and stop at the next gate.

## Research Admission Rule

Research creates evidence and options. It does not automatically change canon. Canon adoption requires an architecture/status decision recorded in the repo.

## Controlled Brief Requirements

A chapter brief should include:

- source authority files read;
- chapter job statement;
- required scene pressure;
- continuity locks;
- what must be withheld;
- what must change by the end;
- explicit stop condition before prose if prose is not authorized.

## Pitfalls

- Turning a “review/refine” request into architecture or drafting.
- Letting research invent a plot without tying it to current series rules.
- Writing a brief that silently authorizes the next chapter.
- Importing historical case-study gates as if they are current.

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] Authority stack read from the repo.
- [ ] Planning vs prose boundary explicit.
- [ ] Evidence distinguished from canon.
- [ ] Status/handoff updated only within scope.
- [ ] Protected prose unchanged unless separately authorized.
