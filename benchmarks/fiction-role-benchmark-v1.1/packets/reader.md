# Reader Packet

Read the following scene as a paying adult suspense reader. Do not diagnose craft, suggest fixes, or refer to a rubric. Report only your experience and uncertainty.

BEGIN SCENE
Mara touched the water beneath Saye's car. Cold, clean, no oil rainbow. She put one wet finger to her tongue before dignity could intervene.

Fresh.

Above the deck, the storm hammered the port windows. Nothing reached the covered centre lane. Nessa stood near the passenger stairs with both hands hooked through the straps of her red backpack. Saye stood closer to his grey case than he had a minute ago.

"Her ticket," he said. "If we're listing impossible things, start there."

Nessa looked at Mara, not Saye. "Eli sent me a message tonight."

The name took the deck out from under Mara. She checked the ferry clock: 11:48. Her wristwatch said 11:55. Eli's number had been disconnected eleven years.

Three short beeps came from the maintenance panel.

Tom's voice cracked over the intercom. "Mara? I've got my hands full up here."

The brass compass turned once in her pocket. Inland, north, nowhere—she could not tell through steel.

Nessa opened her mouth. Mara raised one hand. Questions could wait eighteen seconds. An open maintenance stair could not.

She crossed the vehicle deck and put her hand on the door lever. Fresh water traced a line from beneath it.
END SCENE
