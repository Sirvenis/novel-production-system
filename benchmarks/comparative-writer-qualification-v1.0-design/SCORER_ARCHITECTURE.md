# Writer Scorer Architecture

## Lanes

Minimum blind scoring lanes per candidate:

1. Fresh Reader — reader experience only.
2. Editor — craft and revision burden.
3. Showrunner — canon/brief preservation and production risk.

A lane may be human, LLM-assisted, or LLM-run depending on Andrew / Arden approval and cost constraints. The scorer type must be recorded. A single profile may not score more than one lane for the same candidate unless Andrew / Arden approve a reduced-cost exception; if that happens, independence is downgraded.

## Calibration

Before a high-stakes scored run, use one non-candidate anchor sample or previously preserved failed sample to calibrate scoring variance. If lane scores vary by more than 15 points on the anchor, record the variance and clarify rubric interpretation before scoring candidates.

## Fresh Reader mini-rubric

Fresh Reader score should record:

- engagement / desire to continue: 1–5;
- clarity / confusion: 1–5;
- emotional connection: 1–5;
- momentum / pacing as experienced: 1–5;
- scene promise / ending pressure: 1–5;
- one-sentence reader reaction;
- top confusion or drop-out point.

These support the 100-point rubric but keep reader response from becoming unanchored opinion.

## Conflict resolution

If scorers disagree by 15+ points overall or invert pass/fail status, Scout records the disagreement and performs a final synthesis. The candidate is not silently averaged into a pass.
