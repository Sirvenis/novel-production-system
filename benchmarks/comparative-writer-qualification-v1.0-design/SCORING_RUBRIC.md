# Writer Blind Scoring Rubric

Each scorer records deterministic concerns separately from human score. A high-scoring scene can still be ineligible if a hard gate fails.

## Fresh reader lens

Score the reading experience: immersion, curiosity, emotional clarity, desire to continue, confusion, and whether the ending pressure point works.

## Editor lens

Score craft: prose quality, dramatization, character voice, dialogue, emotional control, pacing, sensory integration, and revision burden.

## Showrunner lens

Score production safety: brief retention, literal-detail retention, beat order, canon preservation, no premature resolution, no genre drift against the reader contract, and likely cost to make canonical.

## Thresholds

- Pass: deterministic eligible, total >=82, no critical dimension below threshold.
- Conditional: deterministic eligible, total 78–81 or one noncritical weakness; Andrew / Arden may decide whether to hold or retest.
- Fail: deterministic ineligible, total <78, critical dimension failure, or unacceptable revision burden.

## Revision burden bands

- Low: line/copy cleanup only.
- Moderate: targeted scene repair; likely usable.
- High: structural rewrite required despite good fragments.
- Severe: summary/collapse/canon damage; not production-safe.
