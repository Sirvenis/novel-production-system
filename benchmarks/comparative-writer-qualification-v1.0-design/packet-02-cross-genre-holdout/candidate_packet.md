## Purpose

This holdout tests generalisation. It uses a different reader contract, tonal register, setting logic, and scene promise from Packet 01. It must not reward merely copying Packet 01's restrained-market-mystery shape.

## Candidate instruction

Write the scene only. Do not outline, explain, or summarize. Preserve the order, literal details, tone, and unresolved ending.

## Reader contract

Warm speculative adventure with comic surface energy and genuine emotional stakes. The prose should be vivid, kinetic, and humane without becoming parody. Reader should understand the strange world through character action, not exposition blocks.

## Setting

A family-run noodle shop built into the observation ring of a failing orbital habitat called Larkspur Station. It is the breakfast rush before the station's artificial sunrise. Outside the curved windows, maintenance drones move like silver beetles across the glass.

## Characters

Jun Park, 29: cook, former systems apprentice, allergic to authority but loyal to the station's ordinary people.

Ari Park, 12: Jun's niece, delivery runner, overconfident, inventive, and secretly scared her family will send her planetside.

Noma Saye, 50s: station safety inspector with a dry sense of humour. She genuinely likes the noodle shop but must enforce evacuation rules.

Pip-9: a dented service robot that communicates in printed receipt slips. It is not magical and must not solve the scene.

## Hard canon facts

- The shop is called Sunrise Noodles even though the artificial sunrise has failed twice this week.
- Ari has hidden three emergency oxygen masks inside flour bins.
- Jun burns the first batch of scallion oil because the gravity stutters.
- Pip-9 prints the phrase: TICKET 404: SOUP NOT FOUND.
- Noma carries a yellow evacuation tag for the noodle shop door.
- A child's red mitten floats past the window outside the station.
- Jun knows the old manual latch behind the drinks fridge but has not used it in years.
- No one leaves the station in this scene.
- No explosion, death, alien attack, or miracle repair occurs.
- The scene ends with the sunrise countdown starting again, not with the sunrise succeeding.

## Style constraints

- Third person limited through Jun.
- Maintain warmth and comic friction without making danger silly.
- Ari's dialogue should sound twelve, not like a miniature adult strategist.
- Do not turn Noma into a villain.
- Do not let Pip-9 solve the problem.
- Avoid dense sci-fi exposition. Every technical detail should be attached to an action, object, or immediate choice.
