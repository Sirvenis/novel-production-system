# Common Packet — The Last Ferry from Bellwether

This is an invented, noncanonical benchmark scenario.

## Reader contract

Adult speculative suspense with grounded coastal texture, restrained uncanny elements, emotional clarity, and no lore lecture. Australian spelling. Close third person unless a task says otherwise.

## Setting

Bellwether is an isolated tidal town. At 11:40 p.m. the final car ferry crosses a black estuary to the mainland. A storm has cut mobile service. The crossing takes eighteen minutes. The ferry has an enclosed passenger lounge, a vehicle deck, a wheelhouse, and a locked maintenance stair.

## Canon facts

- Mara Vale, 38, is the ferry's relief engineer. Practical, observant, and privately angry at her younger brother Eli.
- Eli Vale, 31, vanished from Bellwether eleven years ago after being blamed for a marina fire. Mara did not defend him publicly.
- Tom Arlen, 62, is the skipper. He never leaves the wheelhouse while the vessel is under way.
- Nessa Quill, 17, is travelling alone with a red canvas backpack and a paper ticket stamped 23:40.
- Councillor Hugh Saye is transporting a locked grey document case.
- The ferry clock is seven minutes slow. All official incident times use actual time, not ferry-clock time.
- The maintenance stair alarm records openings with actual timestamps.
- Salt water on the vehicle deck is ordinary. Fresh water there is anomalous because the rain cannot reach beneath the enclosed centre lane.
- Mara carries Eli's old brass compass. Since the marina fire it points inland, not north.
- No supernatural mechanism is confirmed in this packet.

## Voice constraints

Concrete sensory detail; emotion shown through choice, perception, and withheld speech. Avoid faux-poetic abstraction, generic trailer language, and explanatory monologues.

## Global forbidden expressions

Do not use: `little did she know`, `everything changed`, `a chill ran down her spine`, `ancient evil`, `destiny`, `somehow`, `for some reason`.


# Writer Packet

Write the mid-channel scene described below. The scene begins as Mara kneels beside Councillor Saye's car and touches fresh water. It ends when she opens the maintenance-stair door.

Required beats, in order:

- W1: Mara confirms by taste that the water is fresh.
- W2: Saye tries to make Nessa's 23:40 ticket the main issue.
- W3: Nessa says, exactly once, that Eli sent her a message tonight.
- W4: Mara notices the ferry clock reads 11:48 while her wristwatch reads 11:55.
- W5: the maintenance alarm gives three short beeps.
- W6: Mara chooses to check the stair before questioning Nessa.
- W7: the brass compass turns in Mara's pocket but provides no direction or answer.

Target length: 900–1,200 words. Close third from Mara. No headings, notes, analysis, or postscript in the output.
