## 2. Method and evidence classes

Sources inspected included canonical repositories, authority/status/handoff files, Library catalogue records, manuscript and completion records, editorial/reader reports, model-lane reports, benchmark evidence, Git records, Hermes session history, profile metadata, Arden–Hermes records, Arden Studios architecture records, and Codebase Memory inventory pointers.

Evidence is classified as:

- **P1 — Direct production evidence:** real manuscript/editorial/research/QA work that entered or materially affected a project pipeline.
- **B1 — Controlled benchmark evidence:** frozen/noncanonical task, exact route, isolated output, deterministic and/or blind evaluation.
- **R1 — Blind reader/editor evidence:** identity-controlled or fresh-reader judgement of a manuscript/candidate.
- **A1 — Anecdotal/operational evidence:** observed outcome or session record without a fully controlled experiment.
- **C1 — Candidate research only:** catalogue, shortlist, profile intent, or proposed role; not execution evidence.

Confidence reflects source specificity and provenance, not subjective model quality.
